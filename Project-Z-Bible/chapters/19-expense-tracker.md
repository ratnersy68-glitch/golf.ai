# Expense Tracker

> **📌 Note:** This tracks every individual dollar spent, line by line. Reconcile the
> category totals here against the [Budget Tracker](#budget-tracker) monthly. A reusable
> blank entry template is available at `templates/expense-entry-template.md`.

## Why track payment method, not just amount

> **📌 Note for first-timers:** Payment method matters for two practical reasons: it helps
> you spot how much of the build is financed versus paid outright (relevant to the financing
> guidance in the [Buyer's Guide](#the-350z-buyers-guide)), and it makes reconciling your
> own bank/card statements against this tracker much faster if a number ever looks off.

## Receipts and recordkeeping

> **✅ Checklist**
> - [ ] Keep physical or scanned digital copies of every receipt/invoice over a meaningful
>   amount, not just the row in this tracker
> - [ ] Note whether local tax was charged/applicable, especially for out-of-region online
>   orders — relevant if you ever need to reconcile with tax records
> - [ ] If any purchase might be warranty-relevant later, store its receipt somewhere you'll
>   actually find it again (not a random drawer)

>   categories

## Frequently asked questions

> **Q: Should shipping costs get their own line, or fold into the part's cost?**
> Either works as long as you're consistent — folding shipping into the part's total amount
> is usually simplest and keeps category totals accurate without adding rows.

> **Q: What if I return or exchange a part?**
> Log the original purchase as normal, then add a new row for the refund/credit as a negative
> amount in the same category, with a note referencing the original row — keeps the running
> total accurate without deleting history.

> **Q: I share build costs with a partner/family member — how should that work here?**
> Track total spend here regardless of who physically paid, and settle any split separately —
> keeping one source of truth for "what did the build cost" avoids confusion later, even if
> the money itself is divided differently.
