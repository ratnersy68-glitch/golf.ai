# Expense Tracker

> **📌 Note:** This tracks every individual dollar spent, line by line. Reconcile the
> category totals here against the [Budget Tracker](#budget-tracker) monthly. A reusable
> blank entry template is available at `templates/expense-entry-template.md`.

## How to use this tracker

[Modification Log](#modification-log) example entries — delete them and start your own once

## Monthly reconciliation

> **✅ Checklist — do this every month during an active build phase**
> - [ ] Sum this month's entries by category
> - [ ] Compare against the [Budget Tracker](#budget-tracker) planned amounts for those
>   categories
> - [ ] Flag any category trending over its planned range before the next purchase, not
>   after
> - [ ] Confirm contingency fund is still intact (or note what it's been used for)
