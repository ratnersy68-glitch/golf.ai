# Parts List

> **📌 Note:** This is a category-level shopping list, intentionally brand-agnostic. Use it
> to build your own quote sheet from the shops/brands available in your region. Cross-check
> quantities and fitment against your exact VIN (some parts differ between 2005 and 2006,
> and between Base/Touring/Grand Touring trims).

## How to use this list

1. Copy the relevant section into your own quote request to a shop or parts supplier.
2. Fill in the brand, part number, and price as you get quotes.
3. Log the final purchase in the [Expense Tracker](#expense-tracker) and
   [Modification Log](#modification-log) once bought and installed.
