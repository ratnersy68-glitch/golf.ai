### Welcome, especially if this is your first project car

This book was written so that someone who has never owned a modified car before can read
it front to back and come out the other side able to hold their own in a conversation with
a shop, a seller, or a tuner. Every chapter assumes you might be new to this — vocabulary is
explained the first time it's used (and again in the [Glossary](#glossary) if you forget),
every checklist spells out *why* a step matters and not just *what* to do, and every "how
much will this cost / how long will this take" question gets a real, specific answer instead
of "it depends."

If you only read five chapters before buying the car, make them:
[Beginner Car Guide](#beginner-car-guide), [The 350Z Buyer's Guide](#the-350z-buyers-guide),
[Inspection Checklist](#inspection-checklist), [Project Vision](#project-vision), and
[Budget Tracker](#budget-tracker).
