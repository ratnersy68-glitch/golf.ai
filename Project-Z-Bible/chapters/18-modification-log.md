# Modification Log

> **📌 Note:** This log tracks every performance/cosmetic modification — the permanent
> record of what's actually on the car, matched against the [Parts List](#parts-list). A
> reusable blank entry template is available at
> `templates/modification-log-entry-template.md`.

## Why this log matters more than it seems like it should

> **📌 Note for first-timers:** This is the single document that answers "what is actually on
> this car right now?" — for a future buyer, for a tuner who needs to know what they're
> working with, for an insurance claim, and for you in two years when you've forgotten
> whether that sway bar was the adjustable one or not. Keep it current, not retroactive.

## How to use this log

One row per modification, in installation order. Include part number and phase so the build
history stays reconstructable years later. The rows below are **sample data** — delete them
and start your own log once your build begins.

## Frequently asked questions

> **Q: Do I log a part I removed, not just what I installed?**
> Yes — note removals too ("removed stock sway bar, replaced with X") so the log stays a
> complete, honest history rather than only tracking additions.

> **Q: What if I reverse a modification (e.g., go back to a stock part)?**
> Log it the same way, with a note referencing the original install date/row for context —
> this keeps the full story intact rather than looking like the part was simply deleted.

> **Q: Is this log useful if I never plan to sell the car?**
> Yes — it's just as valuable for your own troubleshooting and planning (e.g., "what stall
> speed converter did we go with, again?") as it is for resale.
