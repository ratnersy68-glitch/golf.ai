# Inspection Checklist

> **📌 Note:** Print this chapter and take it to the pre-purchase inspection, or use it as
> your own PPI (pre-purchase inspection) sheet if you're doing it yourself. Pair with the
> known weak points in the [Buyer's Guide](#the-350z-buyers-guide).

## Before you even start the engine

> **✅ Checklist — walk-around**
> - [ ] Panel gaps even on all four corners (hood, doors, hatch)
> - [ ] Paint depth/thickness consistent across panels (paint meter if available) — flags
>   prior bodywork
> - [ ] Tire brand/wear matches across axle; uneven wear = alignment or suspension issue
> - [ ] Tire date codes (DOT code) — anything over 6 years old should be budgeted for
>   replacement regardless of tread
> - [ ] Frame rails and subframe — no rust-through, no signs of prior impact
> - [ ] Exhaust hangers intact, no dragging or excessive rust-through on the exhaust system
> - [ ] Under-hood: no fresh silicone/RTV in unusual places (sign of a hidden leak repair)
> - [ ] Fluid level and color: engine oil, coolant, transmission, brake, power steering
> - [ ] VIN plate matches title/registration; door jamb sticker present and legible

## Cold start

> **✅ Checklist**
> - [ ] Engine starts within 1–2 seconds of cranking, no extended cranking
> - [ ] Listen for 1–2 seconds of timing chain rattle max — anything longer/louder flags
>   guides/tensioner wear
> - [ ] No blue smoke (oil burning) or black smoke (over-fueling) from exhaust on startup
> - [ ] All dash warning lights illuminate then clear (bulb check), none remain lit
> - [ ] Idle stabilizes within ~30–60 seconds, no excessive hunting

## Engine bay, running

> **✅ Checklist**
> - [ ] No visible oil leaks at valve cover, oil pan, or timing chain cover
> - [ ] No coolant leaks at radiator, hoses, water pump, or reservoir
> - [ ] Belts free of cracking/glazing, no squeal on startup
> - [ ] Idle is smooth — no misfire feel, no rough idle
> - [ ] No unusual knocking, ticking, or rod-knock-like sounds under light throttle blips
> - [ ] AC blows cold, all electrical accessories function (windows, locks, lights, horn)

## Test drive

> **✅ Checklist**
> - [ ] Automatic transmission shifts smoothly through all gears, cold and after 10+ minutes
>   warm
> - [ ] No flare (RPM rise before gear catches) on upshifts, no delayed engagement in
>   Drive/Reverse
> - [ ] No clunk from the differential on deceleration or throttle on/off transitions
> - [ ] No whine that changes pitch with road speed (differential/wheel bearing)
> - [ ] Steering tracks straight, no pulling, no vague/wandering feel
> - [ ] No clunks over bumps (control arm bushings, sway bar end links)
> - [ ] Brakes stop straight, no pulsation (warped rotor) or grinding
> - [ ] No warning lights appear during the drive (VDC, ABS, SLIP, check engine)

## Post-drive checks

> **✅ Checklist**
> - [ ] Recheck all fluid levels for any drop after the drive
> - [ ] Look underneath for fresh drips after the car has been running/driven
> - [ ] Check transmission fluid smell/color if accessible — burnt smell or dark brown color
>   is a red flag
> - [ ] Re-scan for codes — some issues only set codes after a full drive cycle

## Diagnostic scan

> **✅ Checklist**
> - [ ] Full OBD-II scan for stored and pending codes (not just the ones triggering the
>   dash light)
> - [ ] Check readiness monitors — all/most "ready" suggests no recent battery
>   disconnect/code-clearing to hide an issue
> - [ ] Pull freeze-frame data on any stored codes, especially misfire (P0300–P0306) or
>   knock-related codes
> - [ ] Check transmission-specific codes if scan tool supports manufacturer-specific PIDs

## Scoring sheet

| Category | Pass | Minor issue (negotiate) | Major issue (walk or big discount) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body/frame | No rust, even gaps | Minor surface rust, panel gap | Frame rust-through, poor collision repair |
| Engine | Smooth idle, no leaks, brief cold rattle | Minor oil seep, longer cold rattle | Persistent rattle, smoke, misfire |
| Transmission | Smooth shifts, no flare | Occasional harsh shift when cold | Flares, slips, clunks into gear |
| Suspension/Steering | Tight, no clunks | Minor bushing wear | Vague steering, worn-out shocks |
| Brakes | Straight stop, no pulsation | Minor pad wear | Pulsation, grinding, warning light |
| Electrical | Everything works | One minor accessory issue | Multiple failures, unexplained warning lights |

> **🛑 Critical:** Any single item in the "Major issue" column should reset your offer or end
> the negotiation — these are the ones that turn a $22,000 budget build into a $30,000 one.
